# Code of Conduct

This is the English version of the Code of Conduct. [简体中文](/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

### Our Commitment

To foster an open, friendly, inclusive, and project-focused community, we expect all contributors, users, and community members participating in the Class Widgets 2 project to communicate with respect, goodwill, and a constructive attitude.

This Code of Conduct applies to the Class Widgets 2 project repositories and project-related community spaces, including but not limited to GitHub Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, QQ groups, Discord servers, and other communication channels managed or endorsed by the project maintainers.

### Our Standards

We expect community members to:

- Respect other people's opinions, backgrounds, experiences, and contributions.

- Offer comments, suggestions, and criticism in a friendly, professional, and constructive manner.

- Focus on the issue itself when discussing technical matters, rather than attacking or belittling the people involved in the discussion.

- Be open to differing viewpoints and attempt to resolve disagreements through rational discussion.

- Provide information that is as clear, accurate, and helpful as possible when submitting an Issue, Pull Request, or other content.

- Respect the time and effort of project maintainers, contributors, and other community members.

- Report bugs or security issues to the project maintainers responsibly.

The following behaviors are not accepted in the project community:

- Personal attacks, insults, belittling, harassment, or threats directed at others.

- Discrimination or disparagement based on gender, sexual orientation, gender identity, age, race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, appearance, identity, technical skill level, or any other personal characteristic.

- Publishing or distributing another person's private information, or publicly disclosing personally identifying information without their consent.

- Persistent or malicious harassment of others, including stalking, dogpiling, maliciously mentioning or tagging others, or repeatedly sending unwanted messages.

- Publishing or distributing pornographic, extremely violent, hateful, discriminatory, or otherwise clearly inappropriate content in the project community.

- Intentionally disrupting discussions, spamming, maliciously creating conflict, or otherwise interfering with normal communication in the community.

- Impersonating project maintainers, contributors, or other community members.

- Using the project community for fraud, malicious promotion, phishing, malware distribution, or other illegal or harmful activities.

- Any other behavior that clearly violates the basic principles of respect in the community or prevents others from participating normally in the project.

### Maintenance and Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct and will take appropriate and fair action based on the circumstances.

Project maintainers may, depending on the circumstances:

- Remove content that violates this Code of Conduct;

- Close or lock inappropriate Issues, Pull Requests, or Discussions;

- Warn the people involved;

- Temporarily or permanently restrict a person's participation in the project community; and

- Report serious violations to the platform or relevant authorities when necessary.

For minor incidents, maintainers will generally prioritize reminders and communication. For serious, malicious, or persistent violations, they may take restrictive measures immediately.

When handling reports, maintainers will make reasonable efforts to protect the privacy of reporters, the people involved, and relevant witnesses, and will investigate based on the facts. Maintainers may also decline to act on reports that are clearly malicious, lack a factual basis, or concern normal technical disagreements. Enforcement decisions may be reviewed by other project maintainers when necessary.

Maintainers will determine how to handle violations based on the specific circumstances and do not guarantee that every situation will be handled in exactly the same way. For more detailed information about handling community violations, see the [documentation](https://www.yuque.com/rinlit/standards/fx20gsi7w28m1nsr).

### How to Report an Issue

If you experience harassment, threats, malicious behavior, or any other conduct that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it to the project maintainers whenever possible and provide relevant context, screenshots, message links, or other supporting information.

If the situation involves personal privacy or security issues, please avoid posting sensitive information in a public Issue or Discussion.

We will make reasonable efforts to handle reports seriously and promptly while respecting the privacy of everyone involved.

If you believe you have been treated unfairly, you may contact the project maintainers, explain the specific reasons, and request a review. In the event of an immediate risk to personal safety, contact local emergency services or the relevant authorities first.

### Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to the behavior of all project participants in official project spaces, at project-related events, and in other situations where they participate as representatives of the project or where their conduct clearly affects the project community. Project maintainers may also take appropriate action regarding conduct that occurs outside the community but has a significant impact on community members or project collaboration.

This Code of Conduct also applies to public events where project members participate as representatives of Class Widgets 2.
