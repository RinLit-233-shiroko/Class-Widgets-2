## Overview
Class Widgets 2 is the next generation of digital timetable software, rebuilt from the ground up with a newer architecture and a cleaner design language.

Compared to [Class Widgets 1](https://github.com/Class-Widgets/Class-Widgets), this project takes a big leap forward in design, interaction, features, and overall polish.

### Highlights
> This software has not released an official stable version yet.
